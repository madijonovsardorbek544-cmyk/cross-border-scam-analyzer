## Case 001: Fake Account Message

Country or region targeted:
International (students in multiple countries)

Target group:
Students

Scam message:
"Your account has been suspended. Verify now to avoid permanent loss."

Psychological tactics used:
- Fear
- Urgency
- Authority

Language characteristics:
- Short sentences
- Simple vocabulary
- No specific details

Why this scam works:
This scam creates fear by threatening account loss.
It pressures the user to act quickly without thinking.
Students are used to official emails and trust authority messages.

How to detect it:
The message does not include the user's real name.
It does not specify which account is affected.
It pushes immediate action without explanation.
