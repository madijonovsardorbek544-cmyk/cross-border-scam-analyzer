Cross-Border Scam Analyzer (CBSA)

Understanding how online scams adapt across countries and societies

About the Project

CBSA is a student-led cybersecurity project that analyzes how online scams adapt across countries, languages, and psychological contexts. It combines cybersecurity awareness with international relations perspectives.

Case Study: Fake Account Message

Target group: Students (international)

Scam message: “Your account has been suspended. Verify now to avoid permanent loss.”

Psychological tactics: Fear, urgency, authority

This scam adapts across regions by changing the authority it imitates, such as banks, governments, or universities.

Ethics & Purpose

This project is for educational purposes only. It does not collect personal data and focuses on awareness, prevention, and responsible use of cybersecurity knowledge.
