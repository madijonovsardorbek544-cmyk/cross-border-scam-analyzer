Case Study: Case 001 (Summary)

Target: Students (international)

Example message: “Your account has been suspended. Verify now to avoid permanent loss.”

Psychological tactics:

  • Fear (account loss)
  • Urgency (quick action)
  • Authority (official tone)

Cross-border adaptation:

Scammers swap the “authority figure” depending on what people trust locally: banks, government agencies, universities, scholarship programs, or visa services — while keeping the same pressure tactics.
