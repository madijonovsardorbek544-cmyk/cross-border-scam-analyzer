Privacy & Data Policy

CBSA is an educational cybersecurity project focused on scam awareness.

  • Data we collect (optional): email (for login), age group (not exact age), region, interests.
  • Data we do NOT collect: phone numbers, exact birth date, passwords in plain text, sensitive personal details.
  • Storage: data is stored securely in Firebase and tied to your account.
  • Purpose: to personalize educational recommendations and improve scam awareness.
  • Control: you can delete your saved profile data in the dashboard.
  • Ethics: the analyzer runs locally in your browser; messages are not uploaded by default.
