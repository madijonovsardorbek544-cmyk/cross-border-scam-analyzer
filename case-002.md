## Case 002: Scholarship / Visa Pressure Scam

Country/region targeted:
International (students applying abroad)

Target group:
Students + applicants

Scam message example:
"Your scholarship application is incomplete. Pay the processing fee today to avoid rejection. Submit your passport details now."

Psychological tactics used:
- Urgency (today / deadline)
- Authority (fake scholarship/visa office)
- Scarcity (fear of losing opportunity)

Language characteristics:
- Formal tone
- Vague institution name
- Requests sensitive documents/payment

Cross-border adaptation:
- In some countries, scammers pretend to be a visa agency.
- In others, they imitate a university scholarship office.
- The core remains the same: fear of losing a life-changing opportunity.

How to detect it:
- Real scholarship programs rarely ask for fees through random messages.
- Official institutions provide clear identifiers (case/ticket numbers, official domains).
- Requests for passport/ID over chat/email are a major red flag.

Safe action:
- Verify through the university’s official website or official email domain.
- Do not send documents until you confirm the identity through trusted channels.
