Case 001: Fake Account Message

This case shows how the same scam adapts across countries by changing the “authority” it impersonates.

Example message:

“Your account has been suspended. Verify now to avoid permanent loss.”

Psychological tactics:

  • Fear (loss)
  • Urgency (act now)
  • Authority (official tone)

Cross-country comparison

Country/Region Fake Authority Used Main Trigger
United StatesBank or tech companyAccount suspension
EuropeGovernment or tax officeLegal consequences
AsiaUniversity or exam boardAcademic penalties
GlobalOnline service providerImmediate action
